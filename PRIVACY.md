# Chess Dude — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Chess Dude is an offline chess application. **It does not collect, transmit, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.**

## What the app does with your data

- Your games, notes, comments, settings and analysis results are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- The app has **no Internet permission**. It cannot connect to a network, so it has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
- The Stockfish engine and its network file run entirely on your device.
- Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app.

## Data you choose to share

If you use the Share or Export function, the app hands the file or text you chose (for example a PGN, a board image or a FEN) to the Android share sheet. You pick the receiving app. That app's own privacy policy then applies to the data you sent it. When you import games, the app reads only the files you select through Android's document picker.

## Permissions

Chess Dude requests no runtime permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other apps' data.

## Children

Chess Dude is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Google Play

Google Play may collect information about your purchase and installation as described in Google's own policies. We do not receive your personal data from Google.

## Open source

Chess Dude is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later. The complete source code is at https://github.com/abhinavkaiser/chessdude, so every statement above can be checked in the code.
